Cho Ren Sha 68k for the Atari ST

This page makes the files the SidecarTridge Multi-device app needs on its microSD card, in the /CHORENSHA folder: the game's images and its sound for every Atari ST, STE, Mega ST and Mega STE. It works from your copy of the Amiga port's repository, and everything happens in this browser: nothing is uploaded.

1. Get the Amiga port's repository

Download the repository as a zip (about 280 MB, the version the app was tested with).

2. Choose it here

3. Make the card's files

Fetching this release's image pack…

4. Put them on the card

Choose the card itself (its top folder): the files go into its CHORENSHA folder.

Then put the card in the Multi-device, switch the ST on and start GAME.TOS from the cartridge's drive c:.
