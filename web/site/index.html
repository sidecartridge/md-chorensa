Make the card

  1. Choose the repository's zip

    The game's files come from the Amiga port's repository: download it as a zip (about 280 MB, the version the app was tested with), then choose it here.

  3. Put them on the card

    Then put the card in the Multi-device, switch the ST on and start GAME.TOS from the cartridge's drive c:. Everything happens in this browser: nothing is uploaded.
